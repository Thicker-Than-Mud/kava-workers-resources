# Log template

Keep a private, shared log of every violation. One line a day is enough. Patterns win cases.

Keep this log off the bar's Wi-Fi, on your own phone. Save copies outside the bar, in case you lose access to the group chat overnight.

## For every violation, write down

- Date
- Start and end time
- Who was there
- What happened, in facts only, no opinions
- What you were owed, if it was pay or hours

## Example

Date: 2026-11-05
Time: 6:00 pm to 6:45 pm
Who: me, and Sam on barback
What happened: brewed and prepped before open, off the clock. Manager said to come in early and clock in at open.
Owed: 45 minutes at $15.00 an hour.

## Also keep

- Schedules, group chat screenshots, tip pool sheets, pay stubs, photos of the tip jar.
- Every write-up, every schedule change, every time your hours were cut after you spoke up.
- Every incident with a customer: who, when, what was said, who saw it.

Write like the owner will read it: facts only.
