# Who's got your back

## EWOC

Free training and a volunteer organizer matched to your bar. Start here.

workerorganizing.org

## Workers United

The SEIU union behind Starbucks Workers United. Organizes cafes and bars.

workersunited.org

## UNITE HERE

The hospitality union: food service, hotels, bars.

unitehere.org

## Labor Notes

Organizer trainings, guides, and a big yearly conference.

labornotes.org

## NLRB

File a charge or an election petition. You generally have 6 months from a retaliation to file a charge.

nlrb.gov, 1-844-762-6572

## US DOL Wage and Hour

Stolen tips, off-clock work, unpaid wages.

dol.gov/agencies/whd, 1-866-487-9243

## A note on Florida

In Florida, the minimum wage is $15.00 an hour. Tipped workers get $11.98 plus tips, and if tips do not get you to $15 every hour, the bar owes the gap. These numbers change. Check with an organizer before you rely on them.
